Features

Your Personal Privacy Shield

Built with a powerful background analysis engine that works silently in the background, every tab, every site.

🔍

Auto-Discovery

No need to find the Privacy Policy yourself. The extension automatically scans every page for legal links and fetches them in the background.

🤖

Gemini AI Scoring

Google Gemini reads the full privacy policy text and returns a 0–100 privacy score with specific red flags — in seconds.

🔔

Link Highlighting

If a site can't be read automatically, T&C Digest scrolls the page and highlights the Privacy Policy link, asking you to click it.

🏷️

Live Badge Score

A color-coded score badge sits right on the Chrome toolbar — green, amber, or red — so you never have to open the popup to get a quick read.

🔄

Re-Analyze On Demand

Hit the refresh icon in the popup header to instantly wipe the cache and force a fresh AI analysis without reloading the page.

⚡

Smart Caching

Results are cached per-domain so the same analysis isn't repeated on every page visit, keeping API usage efficient and fast.

How It Works

Privacy Analysis in 3 Steps

From page load to scored result — completely automated.

1

You Visit a Site

The moment you open a new tab, T&C Digest starts scanning for Privacy Policy or Terms links in the background.

2

Gemini Reads the Policy

The legal text is sent to Google Gemini AI, which analyses it for data selling, tracking, deletion rights, and more.

3

You Get a Score

A 0–100 privacy score, verdict, and specific red flags appear in the popup and the toolbar badge — before you even sign up.
